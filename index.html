A note in the margins

Art history is a conversation.

Movements are not neat boxes, but shared ways of looking—shaped by the people, places, materials, and upheavals of their time. Artists borrowed, rebelled, and reinvented; many worked across categories or outside them altogether.

This timeline is an invitation rather than a complete map. Its dates are approximate, its stories overlap, and the artworks included are just a few bright windows into a much wider world.

Keep looking, and the story keeps changing. ♡
